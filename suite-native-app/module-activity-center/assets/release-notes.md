### 🚀 New features

- Sign & verify added to the asset detail screen, so you no longer have to go through Account settings.
- View options now include a Hidden tokens screen, listing your hidden and unrecognized tokens across all accounts.
- Asset price chart added to the asset detail screen. Switch between time ranges to track price movements over time.
- Balance and Earn sections added to asset detail screen. See your total holdings across accounts, along with available and staked balances, all in one place.
- New asset-first global receive flow, with improved search and network filters that make finding the right asset or account simpler.
- Sign transactions using Solana's newer v1 transaction format, with support for the latest firmware.
- Import QR codes directly from your photo gallery, without needing to scan them with your camera.
- Scan payment QR codes to automatically fill in the recipient address and amount when sending ETH or ERC-20 tokens.

### 🎨 Improvements

- Arc now appears alongside other EVM networks in network settings, making it easier to find.
- Network filters now support all available networks, making it easier to find and organise your assets.

### 🔧 Bug fixes

- Updated Zcash transaction signing to support the NU7 network upgrade, helping prevent transactions from being rejected.
- Fixed an issue where passphrase-protected wallets could be incorrectly labelled as standard wallets when passphrase entry was enforced on the device.
- Fixed an issue where the same standard wallet could appear multiple times when switching between wallets.
- Minor bugs and usability improvements across the app.
