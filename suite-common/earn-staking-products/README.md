# @suite-common/earn-staking-products

The staking products: providers, network kits, the per-network product factories and `createStakingProductsCompositionRoot`, which builds the adapters the product repository registers. Products implement the contracts from `@suite-common/earn-staking-common` and receive everything an app owns, such as Connect, as injected dependencies. Staking API data comes from the generated client in `@suite-common/earn-staking-api`, whose schemas are the single source of truth; product factories take the client functions they use as a dependency, so tests mock them with `createMockDeps` and the composition root passes the real ones.

`src/providers` is the provider registry: every provider as parsed `stakingProviderSchema` data with its status, links and on-chain identities per network symbol, plus `getStakingProvider` and `findStakingProviderByIdentity`. The identities are this package's own copies; nothing here imports the legacy constants in `wallet-config` or the network packages, which stay until the cleanup removes them.

`src/products/ethereum` holds the Everstake pooled product, `createEverstakePooledProduct({ networkSymbol, ...deps })`, built for `eth` and `thod`: the descriptor with its limits copied from the legacy constants in wei, the product info mapped from `/eth/stats` and `/eth/validators-queue`, and the amount bounds. `src/utils` holds what every product reuses: the amount and position status helpers, the legacy maximum stake formula, the adapter error codes, and the not-implemented stubs a read-only product registers for the write members of the adapter until its kit lands.

`src/products/solana` holds the native delegation products. `createSolanaNativeDelegationProduct({ networkSymbol, provider, messageSystemFeatures, ...deps })` is generic over the validator provider and is built for Everstake on `sol` and `dsol`; `createSolanaExternalDelegationProduct` is the read-only product for stake delegated to any validator no registered provider owns, and stays out of account totals. The composition root builds both for each network and lists the validator providers in each external product's `managedProviders`. Positions read both stake-account lists of the account, assign each stake account by its vote account, and carry the stake accounts as `solana-stake-accounts` details; product info is the chain APY from `/sol/stats`.

`src/products/cardano` holds the pool delegation products for `ada`. `createCardanoNativeDelegationProduct({ networkSymbol, provider, messageSystemFeatures, ...deps })` is built for Everstake's pools, `createCardanoRetiredDelegationProduct` for the retired FiveBinaries pools, and `createCardanoExternalDelegationProduct` for every other pool and for a registered stake key without one. A registered stake key has one position, owned by the product of its pool: the whole balance as liquid stake, the reward balance, the pool and the DRep as `cardano-delegation` details, and the `outdated-provider` and `missing-drep` warnings. All three offer the stake-key operations unstake, claim and vote; only Everstake offers staking. Product info lists Everstake's pools from `/ada/pools` with their APY and saturation.

Only the two application composition roots, in `@trezor/suite` and `@suite-native/state`, depend on this package. Everything else reaches products through the injected product repository from `@suite-common/earn-staking`. `forbiddenDeps.config.ts` enforces both directions, and `@trezor/eslint` keeps the import out of every file but the composition roots themselves.
