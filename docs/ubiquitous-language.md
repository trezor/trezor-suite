# Ubiquitous Language

See [ADR-002](./adr/002-ubiquitous-language.md).

| Name            | Identifier                                            | Example                | Explanation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Deprecated                       |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| `Network`       | `NetworkSymbol`                                       | `btc`                  | A specific blockchain network, such as Ethereum mainnet or Sepolia. Mainnet and testnet are separate networks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | `coin`, `blockchain`             |
| `NetworkFamily` | —                                                     | `bitcoin`              | A group of networks sharing a protocol model and common implementation, such as the Bitcoin family containing Bitcoin (`btc`) and Litecoin (`ltc`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | `NetworkType`                    |
| `Asset`         | `<NetworkSymbol>` or `<NetworkSymbol>-<TokenAddress>` | `btc`, `eth-0x12...78` | Anything an account holds and transfers on a network: either the network's NativeToken or a Token. Use it where both are handled alike, such as balances, amounts, prices, icons, and asset pickers, and narrow to NativeToken or Token only where their rules differ. A NativeToken is identified by its NetworkSymbol, a Token by its NetworkSymbol plus TokenAddress; a ticker alone is insufficient. | `coin`, `currency`               |
| `NativeToken`   | —                                                     | `BNB`                  | A network's base Asset, such as BTC on Bitcoin, ETH on Ethereum or BNB on BNB Smart Chain (`bsc`). Each network has exactly one; it belongs to the network but is a distinct concept, and its ticker can differ from the NetworkSymbol.                                                                                                                                                                  | `coin`, `nativeCoin`             |
| `Token`         | `<NetworkSymbol>-<TokenAddress>`                      | `eth-0x12...78`        | Any Asset on a network other than its NativeToken, such as USDC on Ethereum or on Solana, however the network implements it. Identify it by its network plus its asset identifier (contract address, mint, or equivalent); a ticker alone is insufficient.                                                                                                                                               | `coin`, `ERC20`                  |
| `SignVerify`    | —                                                     | —                      | Signing a message with an account's key to prove control of its address, and verifying such a signature against an address. Its two operations are `signMessage` and `verifyMessage`. The UI calls it "Sign & verify".                                                                                                                                                                                   | `MessageSigner`, `SignAndVerify` |
